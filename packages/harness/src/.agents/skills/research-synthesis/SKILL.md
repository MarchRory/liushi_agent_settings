# Research Synthesis

## Core Rule

Prefer primary sources and current official documentation. Do not present uncertain or outdated claims as verified.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/workflow.md` for source priority and synthesis rules.
2. Use `references/output-schema.md` for the required research summary.
3. Use `examples/standard-output.yaml` as a compact synthesis template.

## Resource Map

- `references/workflow.md`: source priority, comparison rules, and uncertainty handling.
- `references/output-schema.md`: canonical `research_summary` schema.
- `examples/standard-output.yaml`: minimal valid research summary.
